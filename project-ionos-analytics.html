The Challenge

Fragmented Data, Invisible Competitors

The IONOS Domains, Hosting & SSL product team served over 6 million customers across Europe, but made product decisions with fragmented, disconnected data. Adobe Analytics, Elastic APM, and Looker Studio each held critical information, but there was no unified view, and reporting required manual effort across all three systems.

Alongside this, there was no systematic competitive intelligence process. Product priorities were set without a structured view of how 15+ EU market players (Hostinger, GoDaddy, STRATO, 1&1 and others) were positioning, pricing, or differentiating.

Additionally, the team needed clarity on emerging regulatory requirements, specifically eIDAS QWAC compliance for certificate products, and strategic input for the STRATO brand's expansion into Sweden.

My Role

Working Student, Product Management & Business Analysis

I operated as an embedded PM and analyst within the product team, responsible for building the data infrastructure, running the competitive intelligence programme, writing PRDs, creating UX wireframes, and collaborating on GTM assets with the marketing team.

I also designed and implemented AI-enabled workflows using Anthropic Claude and MCP integrations to automate parts of the competitive intelligence process, reducing research cycle time from two days to under four hours.

Approach

Four Workstreams Running in Parallel

1

Unified Analytics Dashboard

Designed a Power BI workspace integrating Adobe Analytics (front-end behaviour), Elastic APM (infrastructure performance), and Looker Studio exports into a single management view. Eliminated manual cross-system reporting, cutting decision lag by 30% and reducing reporting effort by approximately 40%.

2

Competitive Intelligence Programme

Benchmarked 15+ EU competitors across pricing models, product bundling, UX patterns, and regulatory positioning. Built a structured competitive analysis framework that was used directly in Q1 2026 roadmap prioritisation sessions. Automated parts of the research pipeline using Claude MCP integrations.

3

Product Management Activities

Wrote PRDs translating analytical findings into actionable product requirements. Created UX wireframes for proposed features. Collaborated with the marketing team on GTM assets and enablement materials. Contributed to backlog refinement sessions with feature prioritisation input.

4

Regulatory & Market Expansion Research

Mapped eIDAS QWAC requirements and their implications for IONOS certificate product positioning ahead of EU compliance deadlines. Conducted market entry research for STRATO Sweden including customer segment analysis, localisation requirements, and competitive landscape in the Swedish hosting market.

Key Learnings

What This Project Taught Me

Connected data unlocks better questions

When product, marketing, and infrastructure data live in separate tools, the real insight always sits in the gaps. Building the unified view was not just a reporting improvement — it changed the quality of decisions the team could make.

AI amplifies research, not replaces it

The Claude MCP integration reduced research cycle time from 2 days to 4 hours, but the value came from knowing what to look for, how to structure the output, and how to translate findings into product decisions. Automation works best when the human knows exactly what problem they're solving.

Regulatory context is competitive advantage

Understanding eIDAS QWAC requirements ahead of enforcement gave the product team time to shape positioning proactively rather than react. PM value often lives in seeing what is coming before everyone else does.
